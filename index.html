The Official NAEMSS eLibrary Platform

Your Digital Gateway to Earth Sciences

The NAEMSS eLibrary is a premier digital platform dedicated to empowering students in Earth and Mineral Sciences across Nigerian higher education institutions. It provides seamless access to an extensive collection of world-class resources, advanced tools, and cutting-edge research platforms tailored specifically for the geosciences community.
Whether you're engaged in field mapping, geological surveys, mineral exploration, remote sensing, GIS applications, data analysis, or advanced data visualization, the NAEMSS eLibrary equips you with the essential materials to excel academically and professionally.
Elevate your geoscience education and contribute to Nigeria's sustainable mineral resource development—join the NAEMSS eLibrary today and unlock your full potential in the Earth and Mineral Sciences.

NAEMSS eLibrary

Empowering the Next Generation of Earth and Mineral Scientists through Accessible, World-Class Educational Resources and Innovative Digital Tools.
The NAEMSS eLibrary is committed to advancing geoscience education in Nigeria by providing students with seamless access to premium academic materials, cutting-edge research platforms, and custom-built digital tools. Designed specifically for the School of Earth and Mineral Sciences community, our platform bridges the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical application, fostering excellence in fields such as geology, geophysics, meteorology, marine science, and remote sensing. By curating high-quality, open-access resources and developing exclusive NAEMSS applications, we equip learners with the skills and insights needed to address real-world challenges in mineral exploration, environmental sustainability, and climate resilience—ultimately contributing to Nigeria's scientific and economic advancement.
